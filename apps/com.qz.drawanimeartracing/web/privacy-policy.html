SketchLens: AR Drawing Anime (package com.qz.drawanimeartracing, "the app") is published by [PUBLISHER_NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and which third parties are involved.

1. Summary

The app does not require an account. Camera frames are processed only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. The app uses third-party SDKs, listed below, for analytics, advertising, notifications and payments.

2. Permissions the app uses

PermissionPurpose
CAMERAShows the camera preview so the line-art guide can be overlaid on your paper (AR), and records time-lapse videos. Camera data is processed on the device and is not uploaded. If you decline, Trace mode still works without the camera.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13 and later)Shows notifications (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) about new templates and lessons. The app asks only once, after your first saved drawing; it is optional. You can decline, or turn notifications off at any time in system settings.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 only)Only on Android 9, in a single storage prompt: to save your drawings to the Pictures folder and time-lapse videos to the Movies folder, and to read back the app's own album to restore your Sketchbook. On Android 10 and later the app saves through MediaStore and does not declare these permissions.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATEDownloads templates, lessons and configuration, shows ads, sends analytics, and checks the connection.
AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* (Privacy Sandbox)Declared by the advertising and analytics SDKs: reading the advertising identifier, ad measurement and attribution.
BILLINGBuying Premium through Google Play.
Other permissions declared by SDKsVIBRATE and CHECK_LICENSE (AppLovin ad SDK): haptic feedback in ads and checking the app's Google Play license. WAKE_LOCK (ads and analytics tracking SDK): keeps the device awake briefly so ad and analytics events can be sent. FOREGROUND_SERVICE (WorkManager): runs background tasks such as content sync reliably. com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE (Firebase Cloud Messaging): receives push notifications. BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE (Google Play Install Referrer): identifies the install source for attribution. com.amazon.privacypass.ATTEST (Liftoff Vungle ad SDK): ad fraud prevention. These permissions do not give access to your photos, camera, location or contacts.

The app does not request microphone, location or contacts permissions; time-lapse videos are recorded without sound. The storage permissions are requested only on Android 9, to save drawings to the Pictures folder; on Android 10 and later, photos and videos are written to Pictures and Movies through MediaStore (Android's scoped storage) without this permission. Photos you choose from your gallery are picked through the system photo picker and used only on your device. To show quick share buttons, the app checks whether a few sharing apps (such as Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp, Zalo, Messenger and Telegram) are installed; this check stays on your device and the app does not read your list of installed apps.

5. Your rights

You can: reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings (Google, Ads); revoke the camera or notification permission in system settings; delete saved photos and videos from your device's gallery; clear the app's data or uninstall the app to delete local data. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent; to make a request, contact us using the details below.
