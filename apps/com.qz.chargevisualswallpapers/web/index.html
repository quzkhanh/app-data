Voltaura has no accounts. Open the app and start using it: no sign-up, no login.

The three permissions the app asks for all serve one purpose: showing the charging screen when you plug in. Display over other apps opens it while your phone is locked, ignoring battery optimization keeps the service from being stopped, and notifications show your battery level. The app never reads what’s on your screen.

Videos you turn into effects come through Android’s photo picker, so the app only receives the file you choose and never reads your whole gallery. Downloaded and self-made effects stay in the app’s private storage on your phone.

The app is free thanks to ads and an optional Premium plan through Google Play. We use Firebase, Adjust and Meta for measurement and bug fixing, and we don’t sell your data. Details are in our privacy policy.
