Voltaura (package com.qz.chargevisualswallpapers) is published by [PUBLISHER_NAME] ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the app processes, why it does so, and which third parties are involved.

1. Summary

  • No accounts: you do not need to sign up or log in, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Downloaded effects, effects you create, your favorites and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Videos and photos you use to create effects are obtained through the Android Photo Picker: the app receives only the files you select and does not request permission to read all photos and videos on your device. These files are processed on your device.
  • Battery level and charging status are read on your device only, to display them on the charging screen and to alert you when the battery is full; the app does not transmit this information.
  • The app includes advertising, analytics, crash reporting and in-app purchases provided through the third-party SDKs listed in Section 4. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Permissions the app uses

PermissionPurpose
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (display over other apps)Opens the full-screen charging screen when you plug in your charger, including while the device is locked. The app does not read or record the content of other apps. You grant this permission in system settings and may revoke it at any time; if revoked, the charging screen will not appear automatically.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS (ignore battery optimization)Asks your permission to let the background service that listens for plug and unplug events run without being stopped by the system. You may decline or change this later in battery settings.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USERuns a background service that receives plug and unplug events to open and close the charging screen. The service always shows a notification with the battery level and the active effect.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShows the background service notification, reminders to re-enable a permission that has been turned off, and push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging. Optional; you may decline or turn notifications off in system settings.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRestarts the background service after the device boots or the app is updated, so the charging screen keeps working.
SET_WALLPAPERSets a still wallpaper on the home screen, the lock screen or both, when you choose to do so. Live wallpapers use the app's wallpaper service and are confirmed by you in the Android wallpaper picker.
VIBRATEVibrates when the battery is full, if you turn this option on.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATEDownloads the catalog and files for effects, wallpapers and configuration; displays ads; sends analytics and crash reports; checks connectivity so the app can tell you when you are offline.
WAKE_LOCKDeclared by Firebase and the advertising SDKs: keeps the device awake briefly while background work is processed.
AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* (Privacy Sandbox), com.amazon.privacypass.ATTESTDeclared by the advertising and measurement SDKs: reads the advertising ID and supports ad measurement, attribution and ad fraud prevention.
BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICEReads the install referrer from Google Play to measure where installs come from (Adjust, Firebase).
com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVEReceives push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
BILLING, CHECK_LICENSEPurchases the Premium plan and verifies entitlements through Google Play.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_*), external storage, or system settings (WRITE_SETTINGS). The full-battery alert sound is chosen through Android's built-in ringtone picker.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw your consent; to exercise these rights, contact us as described in Section 9.
