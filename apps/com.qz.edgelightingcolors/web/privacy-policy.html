Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

Edge Lighting Colors ("we", "our", or "app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal identity information such as your name, address, or phone number.

  • Notification Listener: If enabled, the Notification Listener Service only inspects incoming notification package names and color categories to illuminate corresponding edge colors. No notification message text or private communication is ever read, stored, or transmitted.
  • Display Over Other Apps: Used solely to draw the floating border animation on screen.
  • Device Information & Diagnostics: Anonymous diagnostic information, crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics), and advertising identifiers are collected to improve stability and display relevant advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect anonymous data subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.
