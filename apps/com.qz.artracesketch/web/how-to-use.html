How to use AR Trace Sketch

Pick a picture, choose how you want to copy it, and follow the lines with your pencil. You need a sheet of paper, a pencil, and something to hold your phone steady, such as a cup or a stack of books.

1. Pick a picture

You can draw from three sources, each in its own tab at the bottom of the app:

  • Template: browse the Hot templates and the Explore grid. Tap a category chip, or the filter button, to show one theme only, or use the search bar (results appear after you type at least 2 letters). Tap the heart on a picture to save it to your favorites.
  • Upload: take a photo with the camera, or choose a picture from your gallery. Your uploads are kept under Recent upload so you can draw them again; tap Edit to delete one.
  • Text: type a word or a name (up to 30 characters), choose one of 12 fonts, then tap Drawing Now to turn it into lettering you can copy.

Tap a picture to open the preview, then tap Continue.

2. Choose Sketch or Trace

Sketch: draw through the camera

  1. Put your paper on a table. Rest your phone on a cup, a jar or a stack of books about 20 to 30 cm above the paper, with the back camera facing down.
  2. Allow camera access when the app asks. You now see your paper on screen with the picture laid over it.
  3. Move the picture with one finger and pinch with two fingers to make it bigger or smaller, so it fits your paper.
  4. Look at the screen while you draw: follow the lines of the picture with your pencil on the real paper.

Trace: paper on top of the screen

  1. Open the picture in Trace mode. The picture is shown on a white background, so no camera is needed.
  2. Turn on maximum brightness, then place a thin sheet of paper directly on the screen.
  3. Lock the picture so it does not move under the paper, then trace the lines you can see through it.

3. Drawing tools

The toolbar at the bottom of the drawing screen and the buttons at the top right give you these tools (Flash is only in Sketch, Brightness only in Trace):

  • Flash (Sketch): turns the phone light on to brighten your paper.
  • Brightness (Trace): sets the screen to full brightness so lines show through the paper. Brightness goes back to normal when you leave.
  • Flip: mirrors the picture left to right.
  • Contrast: shows the picture in strong black and white, so lines are easier to see.
  • Lock: stops the picture from moving or zooming while you draw. Tap again to unlock.
  • Full screen: hides the toolbars. Tap the exit button to bring them back.
  • Guide: shows the short how-to for the current mode again.

Use the opacity slider to make the picture lighter or darker over your paper. The screen stays on while you draw.

4. Finish and keep your drawing

  1. Tap the check button when you are done.
  2. Choose Take a Photo to photograph your drawing, or Maybe next time to go back to the home screen.
  3. On the result screen, tap Share to send the photo, or Download to save it to your gallery (Pictures / AR Trace Sketch).

5. Library and settings

  • Library keeps your artwork photos, your favorite pictures and the pictures you used recently. Tap a section title to see all its pictures; in Your artwork and Recently Used you can also delete items.
  • Settings lets you change the app language, read the privacy policy and terms, rate the app or share it with friends.

Tips

  • Start with simple pictures with clear outlines, then move to detailed ones.
  • In Sketch mode, keep the phone still: if the picture drifts, tap Lock after positioning it.
  • In Trace mode, thin printer paper works best.
