AR Trace Sketch (package com.qz.artracesketch, "the app") is published by [PUBLISHER_NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and which third parties are involved.

1. Summary

The app does not require an account. The camera preview and the photos you take, upload or create stay on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. The app uses the third-party services listed below for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration and payments. The app does not show notifications.

2. Permissions the app uses

PermissionPurpose
CAMERAShows the camera preview in Sketch mode so the picture can be laid over your paper, and takes photos (a photo to draw from in Upload, or a photo of your finished drawing). Camera images are processed on the device and are not uploaded. If you decline, the rest of the app, including Trace mode, still works.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13 and later), READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED (Android 14 and later), READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and earlier)Lets you browse the photos and albums on your device to choose one picture to draw. On Android 14 and later you can choose "Select photos" so the app sees only the photos you pick. The app reads the photo list only on the device, copies only the picture you choose into the app's private storage, and uploads nothing.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 only)Asked only when you tap Download on Android 9, to save your drawing photo to Pictures / AR Trace Sketch. Android 10 and later save through MediaStore without this permission. If you decline, nothing is saved and the rest of the app still works.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATEDownloads drawing templates and configuration, shows ads, sends analytics and checks the connection.
AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* (Privacy Sandbox)Declared by the advertising and analytics SDKs: reading the advertising identifier, ad measurement and attribution.
BILLINGBuying Premium (ad-free) through Google Play.
Other permissions declared by SDKsVIBRATE and CHECK_LICENSE (AppLovin ad SDK): haptic feedback in ads and checking the app's Google Play license. WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE (Firebase and ads SDKs): let events be sent and background tasks finish reliably. com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE (Firebase Cloud Messaging, included with Firebase): can receive messages from Firebase; the app does not show notifications and does not ask for the notification permission. BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE (Google Play Install Referrer): identifies the install source for attribution. com.amazon.privacypass.ATTEST (Liftoff Vungle ad SDK): ad fraud prevention. DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION (AndroidX): an internal permission that keeps the app's own broadcast receivers private to the app. These permissions do not give access to your photos, camera, location or contacts.

The app does not request microphone, location, contacts or notification permissions. When you tap Download, your drawing photo is saved to Pictures / AR Trace Sketch: on Android 10 and later through Android's MediaStore, which needs no extra permission; on Android 9 after you allow the storage permission above.

5. Your choices and rights

You can: reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings (Google, Ads); change your ad consent choice where the consent form is shown; revoke the camera, photos or storage permission in system settings; delete uploads and artwork inside the app, or saved photos from your gallery; buy Premium to stop ads; clear the app's data or uninstall the app to delete all local data. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent; to make a request, contact us using the details below.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
